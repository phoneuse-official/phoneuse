# Code of Conduct

We want PhoneUse Local to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect people's privacy: do not share anyone's personal data, device details or screenshots without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Using this project or its community to plan spam, fraud, account abuse, or control of devices you are not
  authorized to use.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and
may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through
[phoneuse.com](https://phoneuse.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=code_of_conduct&utm_campaign=phoneuse-local).
Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
